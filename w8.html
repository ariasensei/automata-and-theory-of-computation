01 General Instructions

  1. Form groups of five (5) members. One group will have six (6) members.
  2. Each group will be assigned a topic through drawing lots.
  3. Visit the school library and research your assigned topic. Use at least one book from the library, along with additional references from credible websites.
  4. Include a group photo taken inside the library, showing all members holding the books used as references. This is required.
  5. Include at least one screenshot of a webpage used as a reference. Indicate the website title and URL below the screenshot.
  6. Explain the topic in your own words and properly cite your sources.
  7. Submit your research paper as a printed hard copy.

03 Required Research Paper Format

1. Title Page

Include the research title or assigned topic, subject name, names of all group members, year and section, instructor’s name, and submission date.

2. Introduction

Introduce your assigned topic and explain its importance in Automata and the Theory of Computation.

3. Objectives

State what your research aims to explain or explore.

4. Discussion

Present an organized explanation of your topic. Include:

  • Definitions and key concepts
  • Relevant principles, models, or classifications
  • Examples with explanations
  • Diagrams or illustrations, where appropriate
  • Applications and significance in computing
💡 Use subheadings to organize your discussion and make your explanations easier to follow.

5. Conclusion

Summarize the main ideas and explain what your group learned from the research.

6. References

List all books and websites used, following APA citation style. Include at least one book available in the school library.

04 Appendices: Research Documentation

Appendix A – Library Group Photo 📸

Include a clear photo of all group members inside the library, holding the books used as references. Add a caption identifying the books.

Appendix B – Web Reference Screenshot(s) 🌐

Include at least one readable screenshot of a webpage used in your research. Add the webpage title, URL, and date accessed below each screenshot.
