☁️ Course Description

This course introduces the mathematical foundations of computation. It covers finite automata, regular languages, context-free grammars, pushdown automata, Turing machines, computability, and complexity theory. Students analyze computational problems and understand the theoretical limits of computing systems.

🎯 Course Intended Learning Outcomes

  • ✓Explain the fundamental concepts of automata and formal languages.
  • ✓Design and analyze finite automata and grammars.
  • ✓Evaluate computational problems using Turing machines.
  • ✓Differentiate decidable and undecidable problems.
  • ✓Apply complexity analysis to computational models.
